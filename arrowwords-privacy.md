# Privacy Policy — Arrowwords

**Last updated:** 2026-10-30

This policy explains how the Android app *Arrowwords* (the "App") handles
personal data. Our chess apps have their own policies.

## 1. Who is responsible

Driftspark Labs is the data controller. Contact: **driftspark.labs.app@gmail.com**.

## 2. What we collect

We run no servers of our own and the App has no accounts. We never ask for your
name, email address or contacts.

Your settings, puzzle progress, hints and statistics are stored on your device.
If Android backup is switched on, your phone may include them in your own Google
backup. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

The puzzles ship inside the App, so solving them needs no network.

To keep the App working and to improve it, the App sends crash reports and
usage statistics to Google Firebase (section 3), and it shows ads through Google
AdMob (section 4).

When you install the App from a friend's challenge link, Google Play passes the
challenge in that link (the puzzle id and your friend's time and stars) to the
App on its first start (Play Install Referrer). The App uses it to open that
puzzle, and counts that a challenge was opened (section 3).

## 3. Crash reports and usage statistics (Google Firebase)

The App uses three Firebase services, operated by Google:

- **Crashlytics** sends a report when the App crashes or hits an error: what
  went wrong in the code, the device model, Android version, App version, the
  language and the puzzle that was open, and a random installation id.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** records how the App is used: which puzzles
  are started, solved or left unfinished, with time, stars, mistakes and help
  used (including which word help was asked for); help and hints spent or
  earned; which screens are opened; whether a challenge was shared or opened;
  purchases and how they ended; and your App settings (puzzle and App
  language, theme, grid colours, zoom, text size, whether you own
  *Unlimited*). It also receives the device model, Android version, App version,
  your device language, a country and region derived from your IP address, and
  random App and installation ids. If you allow personalized ads (section 4),
  it may also receive your advertising ID.
- **Remote Config** lets us change some App settings without an update. To
  fetch them, the App sends its installation id, App version and device
  language.

We use this data only to fix crashes, to find puzzles and clues that do not
work, to understand which features players use, and to measure whether our own
advertising for the App works. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not
sell it. The data is linked to random ids, not to your name.

Google processes this data for us as our processor under the Firebase and
Google Analytics data processing terms
(https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms). Google's privacy
policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Usage statistics are linked to
our AdMob and Google Play accounts, so that we see ad and purchase revenue next
to App usage, and may be linked to our Google Ads account to measure our own
campaigns for the App.

**Legal basis.** Crash reports are sent on the basis of our legitimate interest
in keeping the App working (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). In the EU, EEA, UK and
Switzerland, usage statistics that store an identifier on your device are only
collected with your consent, given in the same consent form as for ads (Art.
6(1)(a)). If you do not consent, Analytics receives only signals without an
identifier, which Google uses to estimate totals. Elsewhere, usage statistics
are collected on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the App
(Art. 6(1)(f)). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time under
*Settings → Privacy choices*.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, operated by Google: on the menu screens,
on the results screen after a solved puzzle, and inside the popups for hints
and for starting a puzzle over. Apart from the optional video ads you can
choose to watch for extra hints, the App shows no full-screen ads. To serve and
measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process your advertising ID, IP
address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and
your interactions with ads. Google acts under its own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy. Its list of ad technology partners is at
https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903.

**Legal basis.** In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the App asks for your consent
before personalized ads are shown (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). If you decline,
non-personalized ads are shown on the basis of legitimate interest in funding
the App and preventing fraud (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can change or withdraw your
consent at any time under *Settings → Privacy choices*. You can also reset or
delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

If you buy *Unlimited*, the App stops requesting ads altogether, and usage
statistics no longer use your advertising ID.

## 5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details;
Google tells the App which items your account owns, and tells us which items
were bought and for how much.

## 6. Transfers outside the EU

Google may process ad, crash and usage data in the United States and other
countries. Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European
Commission's standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

## 7. Retention

Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Usage statistics are kept for 14 months and
then deleted; totals computed from them (such as the number of players per
month) may be kept longer. Google retains ad data according to its own policy.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port
your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because
the data is linked only to random ids, we usually cannot find your data from
your name or email; contact us and we will help where we can. Uninstalling and
reinstalling the App gives it new random ids, so later data is no longer linked
to earlier data. Requests about ad data are best sent to Google through the
link above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection
Authority: https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with an
updated date.
